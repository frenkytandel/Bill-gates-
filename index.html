About Bill Gates

Visionary, technologist, and one of the world's greatest philanthropists

William Henry Gates III, known universally as Bill Gates, was born on October 28, 1955, in Seattle, Washington. He co-founded Microsoft Corporation in 1975 alongside his childhood friend Paul Allen, transforming it into the world's largest personal computer software company and changing the way humanity interacts with technology forever.

Gates dropped out of Harvard University in 1975 to pursue his vision of "a computer on every desk and in every home" — a dream that once seemed impossibly ambitious but became reality within a generation. Under his leadership as CEO, Microsoft launched iconic products including MS-DOS, Windows, Microsoft Office, and Internet Explorer, and grew into one of the most valuable companies in history.

After stepping down as Microsoft CEO in 2000, Gates redirected his energy toward global health and development through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, the largest private charitable foundation in the world. He has committed to giving away the vast majority of his wealth to combat poverty, disease, and inequity worldwide. Learn more at GatesNotes.com ↗.

Major Contributions

Defining moments that shaped technology and humanity

💻

Founding Microsoft & the PC Revolution

Co-founded Microsoft in 1975 and licensed MS-DOS to IBM in 1980, placing Microsoft software at the heart of the personal computing revolution. His vision of a PC on every desk became a global reality within two decades.

🪟

Windows Operating System

Led the development of Microsoft Windows, which democratised computing with a graphical interface accessible to non-technical users. Windows went on to power over 90% of the world's personal computers.

📄

Microsoft Office Suite

Championed the creation of the Microsoft Office suite — Word, Excel, and PowerPoint — which became the global standard for business productivity and remain among the most widely used software applications in history.

💊

Global Health through Gates Foundation

The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation has invested over $65 billion in global health, funding vaccines that have nearly eradicated polio, fighting malaria and HIV/AIDS, and improving child and maternal health in developing nations.

📚

Education Reform & Digital Literacy

Funded major education initiatives through the Gates Foundation, including Khan Academy grants, community college scholarships, and programmes that have provided millions of students worldwide access to quality learning resources.

🌍

The Giving Pledge

Co-founded the Giving Pledge in 2010 with Warren Buffett, committing billionaires to donate the majority of their wealth to philanthropy. Over 230 of the world's wealthiest individuals have signed the pledge to date.
